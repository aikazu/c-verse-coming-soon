## Platform

web

## Users

Indonesian cosplayer creators and illustrators considering a C.Card collaboration. They arrive through a shared link, often on a phone, and need to understand the physical object, the collaboration, and the commercial terms before replying to the C.Verse team.

## Product Purpose

This join surface explains the C.Card collaboration for creators and illustrators and helps each visitor choose the appropriate path. It gives them the process, examples, role responsibilities, schedule, rights, and next action. Success is a desirable, understandable collaboration proposal that leads to an informed reply to the C.Verse team.

## Positioning

C.Card turns creator or illustrator work into a limited physical collectible with an NFC identity, creator signature, Vault custody, and example-based primary and resale earnings. Creator Seed is a one-of-one gift; collaboration and resale mechanics distinguish it from ordinary merchandise.

## Operating Context

The site is an interactive companion to the creator deck in `00_Dream_Project/40_operations/decks/creator-20261031/`. Visitors read it before discussing a release with the C.Verse team. No account or checkout is part of this surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Separate creator and illustrator paths, ten sections each, with deep links and browser history.
- Card examples, front/back inspection, calculators for Seed, primary sales and resale royalties, and a copyable reply template.
- Calculations and factual copy match the Dream deck and Platform shared economy rules. Calculator inputs are examples, not promises.
- Every primary-release example uses 30 C.Coin per card. Rounding is upward per transaction.
- Keep join out of search through HTML noindex, X-Robots-Tag, and robots disallow.
- Indonesian UI; responsive and keyboard accessible; reduced-motion preferences preserved.
- Existing static Vite site, separate `join/` build and Cloudflare Worker. No new framework or backend is required.

## Brand Commitments

Use the validated CreatorVerse identity: black `#171717`, lime `#CCFF66`, orange `#FF6633`, silver `#CCCCCC`; one accent per section, with lime and orange not used together. Inter is the supporting face. Existing master logo shapes must not be redrawn or retyped. Product names use C.Verse, C.Card, C.Coin, and C.Gems.

## Evidence on Hand

Real example artwork and credits are in `join/src/assets/cards/`; master brand marks are in `join/src/assets/brand/`. Example designs are not announcements of a release and require the owners' permission. Product claims come from the current join HTML, Dream creator deck, and Platform economy implementation. No fabricated testimonials, demand evidence, payment readiness, or legal approval.

## Product Principles

- Make the physical artwork and collectible understandable before asking for action.
- Let each visitor find their own role without losing the other path.
- Show real commercial mechanics with example labels, not marketing promises.
- Keep rights, schedule, and next steps explicit.
- Preserve existing links, calculator behavior, and search restrictions through visual changes.
